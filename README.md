# Hi, I'm Jourdan Rombough

### AI Search & GEO Strategist · Technical SEO · 15+ years · Builder of interactive SEO tools

I've been hooked on technology for as long as I can remember. Before SEO, I worked in the wireless industry as a Level 3 Technical Support specialist and Roaming Coordinator, solving the problems no one else could.

In my early twenties I fell in love with magic: street magic, close-up card work and tableside performances at restaurants at night. I'd built up a library of performance videos, so I built my first website to share them. That led to the question that changed my career:

> **"How do I get these videos to rank and actually be seen?"**

Answering it started my journey into SEO. More than 15 years later, I've driven organic growth for brands like **McDonald's, Uber and Comcast**, and today I focus on where search is headed: **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** and **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)**, making sure brands are retrieved, trusted and cited by AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity.

The same curiosity that once had me figuring out how to get a card trick seen still drives me: **understand how the system works, then make sure the right content gets seen.**
